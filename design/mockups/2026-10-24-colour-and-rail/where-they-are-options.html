“Where they are”, three ways

Same five applicants in each. The dotted 11-step rail is gone in all three. Shown in brass; the designs work in any colour.

Option A — stage word, then a thin progress bar

You read the stage first. The bar shows how far through the steps they are; the bright segment is the step they are on. The small line under it names the step.
