The actual problem
Green isn't the problem. Green everywhere is.
Right now every single surface in the app is tinted green — the page, the sidebar, the cards, even the hairlines between them. Then the accent that's supposed to stand out is green too. When the highlight is the same color as the wall behind it, nothing can stand out, so the whole screen flattens into one note.
