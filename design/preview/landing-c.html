Before you ask

Fair questions.

What does it cost?

Honestly: simple pricing, announced at launch — no numbers hiding behind a demo call. What we can promise today is this: your first hire is free. If Ava doesn’t earn her keep on hire number one, you owe us nothing.

Are candidates talking to a bot?

They’re talking to Ava, and she says so — she introduces herself as your shop’s hiring assistant and never pretends to be human. It’s a real voice conversation about the actual job, usually about eight minutes, at whatever hour suits them. Applicants tend to like it for the same reason you will: they get answered in minutes, and they’re never ghosted.

I already post on job boards. Do I have to stop?

No — keep whatever works. HireFlow publishes your job to the big job-search feeds automatically, and everything funnels back into one place. Whoever applies, wherever they came from, Ava interviews them the same way.

How fast is it, honestly?

Interviews start minutes after someone applies — the 11 pm applicant is usually interviewed by 11:04. The shortlist builds as good people show up, typically over the first few days a job is live. The slow part of hiring was never the paperwork; it was the waiting. That part’s gone.

What’s actually left for me to do?

Three things. Tell Ava the job. Read a short shortlist. Click Interview on the people you want to meet. She handles the posting, the screening, the scheduling, the reminders and every reply — and she never makes a hiring decision. Those stay yours.
