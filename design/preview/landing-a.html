How it works

Three steps. Only one of them is yours.

01

Tell Ava about the job.

Say it in plain words — the pay, the hours, what a good hire looks like in your shop. Ava turns that into a proper job post and puts it where people already look for work.

02

Ava interviews everyone.

Minutes after each application — any hour, any day — every applicant gets a real screening interview about the actual work. Nobody waits, and nobody gets skipped.

03

You make the call.

Ava seals a short shortlist and shows the evidence behind each score. You pick who to meet and who to hire.

Fair questions

Asked by every owner. Answered straight.

What does it cost?
Simple pricing, announced at launch. Your first hire is free, start to finish, so you can judge Ava on a real hire before paying anything. No per-applicant surprises.
Do candidates talk to a bot?
They talk to Ava, and they know it — we don’t pretend otherwise. It’s a real, structured interview about the actual work, by voice or text, at whatever hour suits them. You can read or listen to every word. A real interview at 9 pm beats a form and silence.
What if I already use job boards?
Keep them. HireFlow writes your post and puts it where people already look, then handles what the boards don’t: the screening, the replies, the shortlist. Applicants from anywhere — boards, your window sign, a shared link — all land in the same morning read.
How fast will I see applicants?
Your job is usually live the day you post it. From there, Ava interviews each applicant within minutes of applying, around the clock, and seals the shortlist as the evidence comes in — so the wait is for applicants, never for paperwork.
Who actually decides who gets hired?
You do. Ava reads, interviews, scores and recommends — she has no reject button and no hire button. Every applicant’s outcome is a click made by you, and the courteous note that follows goes out in your name.
