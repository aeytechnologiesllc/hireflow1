Fair questions

Asked straight, answered straight.

What does it cost?

Simple pricing, announced at launch. What we’ll promise now: your first three applicants are free — post the job, read the shortlist, hire someone, pay nothing. Judge HireFlow by the person you hired, not by a sales page.

Are my applicants talking to a bot?

They’re talking to Ava, and she says so — she introduces herself as your hiring assistant and never pretends to be human. It’s a real conversation about the actual job, by voice or text, at whatever hour they apply. Most applicants prefer a fair listen at 11 pm to a form and three weeks of silence.

I already post on job boards. What changes?

Keep whatever works. HireFlow lists your job where local job-seekers already look, and gives you a link and QR code for the door, the counter, or your socials. Wherever people come from, every one of them gets the same interview and lands in the same shortlist.

How fast is it, really?

Applicants are interviewed minutes after applying, day or night. Your shortlist builds as they come in — most owners are reading sealed briefs with their coffee the next morning, not sorting resumes on Sunday night.

Can Ava turn someone down without me?

No. Never. Ava scores, seals, and recommends — passing on someone is always your click. And when you do pass, she sends a courteous reply in your name, so the answer is a kindness instead of silence.
