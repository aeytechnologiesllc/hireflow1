HireFlow · How a customer flows through the product

From signup to hired — and exactly when money moves

Prices shown are working examples, not locked. Two charge points in the whole journey: publish, and boost — and the boost is only charged once the ad is actually live.

One-time
before launch
(us, never them) HireFlow's own Facebook business account gets verified once, our ad templates get pre-approved, Stripe goes live. Every customer's boosts run from this one account, forever. No customer ever creates, verifies, or connects an ad account.
Sign up· customer, 2 minFree · instant
What they do

Create an account and enter business name, address, phone — required, no exceptions.

Behind the scenes

That's our vetting, not Facebook's. Ava sanity-checks the details in seconds. Nothing is pending, nobody waits.

Create the job· customer + Ava, 5 minFree · instant
What they do

Tell Ava about the role. She writes the post, the screening questions, and the interview flow.

Behind the scenes

Drafting costs us pennies. The expensive AI hasn't started yet — that's the point. A suspicious listing gets held for review here; a normal one never does.

Publish· customer taps onceCharge №1 — e.g. $99 · 30 days
What they do

Tap Publish, pay in the app. That's the whole job posting, done.

Behind the scenes

Live instantly on their hosted page + Google, Adzuna, Jooble, Talent.com. Ava now screens and voice-interviews every applicant — the fee they just paid is what funds her. Cost and revenue arrive together.

Boost — optional· customer taps onceCard held — not charged yet
What they do

See a real reach estimate — “up to ~8,000 people near your shop” (Facebook's own numbers) — pick 3, 5 or 7 days (e.g. $59 / $99 / $149) and tap Boost.

Behind the scenes

Card is authorized, not charged. Software builds the ad from our pre-approved template — their business name in the ad, running from our ad account. They never touch Facebook.

Facebook reviews the ad· automatic, minutes–24 hPer-ad review, never per-business
Approved → liveNow the hold becomes the charge. The customer's screen flips from “Boost in review” to “Boost live — reaching people nearby.”
RejectedAva fixes the wording or image and resubmits automatically. Still not live in 24 h? The hold is released — they never paid — and the job keeps running on the free networks.
Applicants → sealed → hired· Ava works, they decideIncluded in the publish fee
What they do

Wake up to the sealed shortlist. Interview. Say “hired” — the offer letter and paperwork send themselves.

Behind the scenes

Every applicant screened and voice-interviewed within usage caps, every rejection answered politely in their name. Job expires at 30 days or when the role is filled — same rhythm as Indeed.

The money line, end to end

Sign up · $0→ Create job · $0→ Publish · pay once→ Boost · card held→ Ad live · charge lands→ Ad never runs · hold released, $0

Of a $149 boost, roughly a third goes to actual ad spend; the rest covers Ava's screening of the extra applicants the boost brings in, payment fees, and margin — sold as one flat price for an outcome, which is also what keeps us clear of Facebook's rules about managing other people's ad budgets.
