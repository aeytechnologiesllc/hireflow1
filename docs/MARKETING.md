# Getting HireFlow in front of businesses (2026-11-03)

The owner, 2026-11-03: "Nobody knows about us ... Do we run ads? ... How do we
market ourselves?" No outside business has signed up yet (16 business
accounts, 1 in the last 30 days). Google had only the front page
(docs/SEO.md, "Getting into search engines").

**Voice (owner, 2026-11-03):** HireFlow speaks as the company ("we", the
HireFlow team), never the owner's name or personal details. His hiring runs
are "how we hired our own support team". Channels that need no personal
account come first: our blog, free tools on hireflownow.com, Google and Bing.
Accounts elsewhere use a company address (hello@hireflownow.com; the domain
forwards email through Namecheap).

## Who we are for, in one line

Small businesses hiring remote chat or email support agents, most often from
the Philippines. They post on OnlineJobs.ph, get a flood of applicants
(OnlineJobs.ph's Pro plan takes up to 200 applications a job,
[Betterteam](https://betterteam.com/onlinejobs-ph)) and have no good way to
test them. OnlineJobs.ph sells screening only as a paid recruitment service
([Betterteam](https://www.betterteam.com/ph/onlinejobs-ph)). The owner's own
two runs are the proof: 155 applicants in October, 117 in a day in November,
every one tested by a real customer chat, the best few on top.

The pitch: **"Got 200 applicants? HireFlow tests every one with a real
customer chat and puts the best three on your desk. Your first job is free."**

## What to do, in order (pick a few channels and commit)

Advice for a B2B product's first ten customers agrees: the founder sells
personally first, two or three channels at most, and paid ads only once
something converts
([summary of Lenny Rachitsky's interviews](https://claudeskills.info/skills/refoundai/lenny-skills/first-b2b-customers/),
[Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/s/b2b-saas-marketing-strategy),
[Causo](https://hub.causo.ai/guides/first-100-paying-customers-channel-mix-2026)).

1. **Reach businesses that are hiring support agents right now.** They have
   the problem today. Find them on LinkedIn and Indeed (small companies
   posting "remote customer service", "chat support"), and in the Facebook
   groups where employers hire Filipino VAs. Send a short, personal message
   from the owner: his own story, the free first job, an offer to set the job
   up for them, signed as the HireFlow team. About ten a day. This is the fastest path to the first
   customers.
2. **Post the story where owners ask about hiring.** Reddit (r/smallbusiness,
   r/ecommerce, r/shopify, r/Entrepreneur), Facebook groups for employers of
   Filipino VAs, LinkedIn, Indie Hackers. A story, not an ad: "I got 117
   applicants for a chat job in a day and didn't read a résumé." Read each
   group's rules first. Links always tagged (`?utm_source=reddit&utm_campaign=story1`).
3. **Podcasts about hiring Filipino VAs.** John Jonas of OnlineJobs.ph has been
   a guest on My Wife Quit Her Job, EcomCrew, Entrepreneurs on Fire, Side
   Hustle School and others ([MWQHJ](https://mywifequitherjob.com/john-jonas-onlinejobsph/),
   [EcomCrew](https://www.ecomcrew.com/e154-how-to-hire-filipino-vas-with-john-jonas-of-onlinejobs-ph/)).
   The same shows take guests with a story like the owner's.
4. **Google, slowly.** Search Console is set up; finish the indexing requests,
   then one article a week aimed at what these owners search ("how to screen
   virtual assistant applicants", "customer service test for hiring"). Content
   brings real inbound in 6 to 12 months, not weeks.
5. **Free listings for links and trust.** Product Hunt, G2, Capterra,
   SaaSHub, AlternativeTo, and the free AI-tool directories (many old lists
   are dead or now paid; check each:
   [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/free-ai-tool-directories-to-submit-your-tool/),
   [WitsCode, checked 2026-10](https://witscode.com/resources/saas-launch-playbook/ai-tool-directories)).
   The owner makes the accounts; the copy is written for him.

## Ads: not yet

Search clicks average $5 to $6 across industries in 2026, HR software higher
([Enrich Labs](https://www.enrichlabs.ai/blog/average-cpc-by-industry-google-ads-2026)).
With no proof yet that a visit turns into a sign-up, $1,000 buys about 170
clicks and maybe a few sign-ups. Run ads once steps 1 and 2 show what makes
people sign up: then a small Google Ads test ($300 to $500) on the exact
searches above, and only keep what pays.
